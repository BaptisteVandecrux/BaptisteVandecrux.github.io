## Education and Professional Experience

### Since 2024 – Senior Researcher, Geological Survey of Denmark and Greenland (GEUS), Copenhagen
*(Researcher, 2019–2024)*
- **[LIQUIDICE](https://eu-liquidice.eu/)**: Work Package 1 Leader, coordinating 20+ partners from 10 institutions in the acquisition, harmonization, and integration of snow, ice, permafrost, and hydrological datasets to monitor how the melting of the cryosphere affects water resources.
- **[Water Under Snow Cover ESA CCI Fellowship](https://climate.esa.int/en/about-us-new/fellowships/esa-cci-research-fellowship-Baptiste-Vandecrux/)**: Remote sensing of liquid water under the Greenland ice sheet.
- **[GC-Net and PROMICE](https://promice.org/)**: AWS deployment, station maintenance, data analysis. PIs: Robert S. Fausto and Anne Solgaard.
- **[SUMup collaborative database](https://doi.org/10.18739/A2M61BR5M)**: Steward of the largest compilation of surface mass balance, subsurface temperature and density measurements from the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets. Coordinating 7.7M data points from 117 contributing authors from 85 institutions.
- **[LiquidIce](https://eu-liquidice.eu/)**: Instrument deployment on the ice sheet, development of novel data processing. GEUS PI: Andreas Ahlstrøm.
- **Thawing Mountains**: Snow cover impact on permafrost temperatures. PI: Michele Citterio.
- **Hydropower Investigations in Southwest Greenland**. PI: Kristian K. Kjeldsen.
- **[SICE (Sentinel-3)](https://snow.geus.dk/)**: Snow characteristics retrieval. PI: Jason E. Box.
- **[Camp Century Climate](https://www.campcenturyclimate.dk/)**: Firn evolution modeling. PI: William T. Colgan.

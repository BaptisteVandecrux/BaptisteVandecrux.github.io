My first ever expedition on the ice sheet! As part of the FirnCoVer project, we were dropped off at DYE-2 with all essentials for a self-supported expedition: skidoos, fuel, camping gear, and science equipment. From there, we spent about 10 days traversing across southern Greenland by snowmobile, stopping at key sites to drill firn cores and service compaction stations. We then transitioned to a ski-equipped aircraft to reach remote research sites including Crawford Point, Summit, and EastGRIP, where we continued our firn and snow investigations.

---

In this fieldwork, we first got dropped at DYE-2 with our scientific equipment, skidoos, fuel and camping kit. Then we traversed for about 10 days to different study sites in South Greenland then took a small ski-equipped airplane to visit more remote sites: Crawford Point, Summit, EastGRIP. At each of these sites, we serviced firn compaction stations, drilled firn cores and made snow pit studies.
